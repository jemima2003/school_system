About ElimuHub

ElimuHub is a school management platform built for CBC schools in Kenya. We help schools replace scattered paperwork and manual record-keeping with one simple, secure online system.

Our goal is to make it easy for teachers to manage results, for school offices to track fees, and for parents to stay informed about their children's progress — all from one place, accessible on any device.
